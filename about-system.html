Heatwave Intelligence System

About the Portal & Operational Objectives

"Our early-warning network bridges the crucial gap between raw meteorological data and immediate disaster management response. Timely heat advisories save lives, safeguard public health, and protect local agricultural yields."
— Dr. A. R. Sharma, Chief Meteorologist & Director of Disaster Warning Operations

1. System Objective

The primary mission of the Heatwave Intelligence Portal is to provide real-time thermal monitoring and automated risk assessment across affected sectors. By integrating high-resolution temperature telemetry, the system enables proactive emergency mitigation and ensures critical advisories reach vulnerable populations during peak summer months.

2. Primary Data Sources

Thermal telemetry is gathered from a nationwide network of Automated Weather Stations (AWS) operating under the IMD, geostationary satellite radiometers, and field sensors. Data feeds update every 15 minutes to ensure accurate heat-index projections up to 7 days in advance.

3. Key Stakeholders & User Groups

This early-warning portal serves three major operational categories: Meteorologists analyzing heatwave trends, Disaster Management Officers coordinating emergency response measures, and the General Public taking preventive actions to avoid heat stroke and severe dehydration.
